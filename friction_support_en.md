# Friction - Help & Support

Welcome to the Friction Help Center. We are here to help you stay focused and reclaim your time.

## Need help with the app?
The fastest way to get support is directly through the application:
1. Open **Friction**.
2. Go to **Settings**.
3. Tap on **Contact Support**.

This allows us to receive the necessary information to help you resolve the issue as quickly as possible.

## Direct Contact
If you are unable to access the app or have a general inquiry, you can contact us via email. Please include your device model and a brief description of the issue.
