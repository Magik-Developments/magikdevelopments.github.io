**Privacy Policy**

This privacy policy applies to the Friction app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by Adria Fernandez Arans (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a Free service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

**Information Collection and Use**

The Application is designed to work locally on your device whenever possible. The Service Provider does not require you to create an account and does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

The Application collects certain information automatically when you download and use it. This information may include:

*   Your device's Internet Protocol address (e.g. IP address)
*   The operating system you use on your mobile device

The Application does not gather precise information about the location of your mobile device and does not use Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies to process your data.

**Sensitive Permissions and Data**

To provide its core functionality of blocking apps and measuring your steps, the Application requires access to specific system APIs. We want to be completely transparent about how these are used:

*   **Health and Activity Data (Steps):** Friction requests access to your physical activity data (via Apple HealthKit on iOS or Google Fit/Health Connect on Android). We use this data solely to count your steps and unlock your selected applications. We do not sell, transfer, share, or use your health data for advertising or marketing purposes. This data is processed locally on your device.
*   **App Blocking and Screen Time Permissions:** To restrict access to your chosen apps, Friction uses native system APIs (such as Screen Time API on iOS or Accessibility/Usage Stats on Android). We use this access exclusively to block and unblock the applications you have selected. We do not collect, transmit, record, or store your screen content, browsing history, or messages.
